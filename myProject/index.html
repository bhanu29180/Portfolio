Telepresence and Teleaction in Robot Assisted Dentistry

Dec 2021 – Jul 2022 | Link

  • Interface the UR5 manipulator and Phantom Touch haptic devices with ROS (Robot Operating System).
  • Position control and trajectory tracking of the end-effector using Jacobian-based control.
  • Developed a program to control the motion of the robotic manipulator using a 6-DoF haptic device.
  • Workspace confinement and force feedback on the haptic device.
  • Estimation of time delay between the input position given by the haptic device and the output end-effector position.

Dynamic Load Analysis of an Oscillating Rotor

Sep 2021 – Dec 2021 | Link

  • Designed an adjustable test rig and mounted it with a 6-DoF load cell, servo actuator, and optical encoder interface using Arduino Mega.
  • Load measurements were performed at various rotor RPMs and oscillation frequencies.
  • Forces were also observed along the direction in which the component of rotor thrust was zero.
